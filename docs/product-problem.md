# The product problem

A person receives INTP one month and INTJ the next. The label is easy to remember, but it does not explain whether their responses changed substantially, whether two candidates were already close, or whether they answered different questions in different circumstances.

TypeSignal addresses this interpretation gap. Its commercial product combines a free Quick assessment with fuller assessment and deeper interpretation, nearby-type explanations, and historical comparison where suitable records are available. The design emphasizes what a result can support instead of presenting one categorical label as a complete description of a person.

The central product questions are:

- Which tendencies are close enough that the displayed label deserves a cautious reading?
- Which answers contribute to the interpretation?
- What changed between comparable assessments, and what remains unknown?

The tradeoff is deliberate: a simple label helps people enter the experience, while additional explanation introduces nuance without promising certainty. A personality self-reflection tool is not a clinical diagnosis or evidence of scientific validation.

This showcase demonstrates only selected engineering boundaries using fictional data. It cannot administer TypeSignal, calculate a type, or reproduce a paid report. See [architecture](architecture.md) for that distinction and [explainability](explainability.md) for the interpretation principles.
